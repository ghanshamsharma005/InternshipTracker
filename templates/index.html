Comprehensive Platform Capabilities

Built for Every Stage of the Academic Internship

Six integrated modules engineered to replace manual spreadsheets, fragmented emails, and paperwork with an auditable institutional system.

1. Internship Management

Submit and maintain corporate placement records with verified employer profiles, workplace mode (Online, Offline, Hybrid), supervisor contacts, and duplicate submission checks.

2. Timeline Tracking

Monitor end-to-end milestone sequencing in real time. Automatic timeline generation ensures transparent checkpoint status from registration through mentor review to completion.

3. Progress Monitoring

Submit weekly activity logs with hours worked, tasks completed, skills acquired, and hurdles faced. Mentors provide structured inline feedback directly on each submission.

4. Document Management

Centralized repository for Offer Letters, Joining Letters, Weekly Logs, and Completion Certificates with file type validation, size checks, and path-traversal protected delivery.

5. Deadline Management

Automated submission due-date calculations and dynamic countdown timers ensure students stay ahead of deadlines and notify faculty mentors of overdue submissions.

6. Faculty Evaluation

Objective 5-metric scoring rubrics grading Technical Skills, Communication, Punctuality, Work Quality, and Discipline (0–10 scale) with instant scorecard publication.
